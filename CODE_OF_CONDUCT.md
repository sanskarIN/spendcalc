# Code of Conduct

SpendCalc is intended to be a welcoming, constructive open-source project.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- give specific, actionable technical feedback;
- welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds;
- focus criticism on code, design, documentation, or process rather than people;
- respect privacy and avoid posting another person's private information;
- stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as disruptive to productive collaboration.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of using a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed with attention to privacy, context, impact, and project safety. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project-managed communication spaces, and interactions where someone is representing SpendCalc.
